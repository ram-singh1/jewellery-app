Jewellery care

  • Put jewellery on last and take it off first — after perfume, lotion and hairspray.
  • Remove before swimming, bathing, exercising or cleaning.
  • Clean with warm water, mild soap and a soft brush; dry with a lint-free cloth.
  • Pearls: wipe with a soft dry cloth only, and store flat.
  • Store each piece separately in its pouch to avoid scratches and tangles.

Gold & materials

Gold purity describes how much pure gold an alloy contains:

  • 22K — 91.6% gold. Rich colour; softer, traditional choice.
  • 18K — 75% gold. The balance of colour and durability for fine jewellery.
  • 14K — 58.5% gold. Most durable; ideal for everyday stacking pieces.
