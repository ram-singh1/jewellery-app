All photographs are served from the Unsplash CDN (`https://images.unsplash.com/photo-<ID>`) and cropped and resized with URL parameters (`w`, `h`, `fit=crop`, `crop=focalpoint`, `auto=format` for WebP/AVIF).

**Licence:** [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). Free for commercial use, with some restrictions (for example, you can't sell the photos unaltered or build a competing image service). Before launch, open each photo on unsplash.com, record the photographer's name and check the licence is still current.

**Important:** these are *stand-in* images. Each product listing must use real photographs of the actual piece before the site sells anything.
